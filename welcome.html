You’re set. Open any Copart lot.

The panel opens on the right of every Copart lot page, and its first check runs by itself — no account, no key, no cost.

Has this car been through auction before?

The answer is at the top: sold before, ran without selling — with the final bid of each run where the auction archives hold it — or no record found. The archive page is one click away, so you can check us rather than trust us.

Who is selling it, and what are the papers?

Seller type (insurer, lender, rental fleet, dealer), title brand with a hard warning on titles that can never be registered, document status, run and drive, and whether the seller can refuse your winning bid.

How does this model do at auction?

How many of this model ran in the last month and how many failed to sell, counted from our nightly snapshots of Copart’s whole calendar. Counts, never a valuation.

Try it now

Pick any car from today’s insurer-sold lots and open it on Copart. On a search page, the badges name the insurer on every row, and one switch hides everything else.

Tip: click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome’s toolbar and pin the extension, so its settings are one click away.

AI photo inspection — optional

Add your own Claude or Gemini key in the extension’s popup and it will read the listing photos for overspray, panel gaps, mismatched paint and flood residue. About a cent or two per inspection, paid to the provider — never to us. Your key stays in your browser.
