Privacy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Insurance Lots is published by tisch-soft. Questions or requests about your data: tisch_games@proton.me.

In short

We use Google Analytics to learn which pages help people, where visitors come from, and whether they come back. It sets cookies.

In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it runs only after you press “Accept”. Elsewhere it runs unless you switch it off under “Cookie settings” at the foot of every page.

Nothing is used for advertising, and nothing is sold.

If you sign up for the free beta, we keep your email address to send you the key and our Sunday newsletter.

Google Analytics

What it records: the pages you view, the site that sent you, your approximate location (country and city, worked out from your IP address, which Google Analytics 4 does not store), your device, browser and language, how long you stay, how far you scroll, and which links to other sites you click.

Cookies: _ga and _ga_HBVQNE95GF, which tell a returning browser from a new one. They last up to two years unless you delete them or press “Reject” under “Cookie settings”, which deletes them.

Switched off: Google signals and ad personalisation. No advertising cookies are set.

Kept: Google keeps this data for up to 14 months, then deletes it.

Provided by Google (Google Ireland Limited for visitors in Europe, Google LLC elsewhere). Data may be processed in the United States, where Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis: in the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time under “Cookie settings”; elsewhere, our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used.

The free beta

If you sign up on the site, we keep your email address (encrypted), your language, the site or post that sent you, when you signed up, and on which days your key opened the list. We use them to send your key, to send our newsletter every Sunday (last week’s market outlook), to see whether the list is used, and to email you about the list, including before paid plans start. Nothing is shared or sold.

The email is delivered by our email provider on our behalf. Every email has a link that stops further emails; your key keeps working. To have your data deleted, write to the address above.

Legal basis: providing the list you asked for, and our legitimate interest in knowing whether it is used.

Kept only in your browser

Your light or dark choice (il-theme) and your cookie choice (il-consent, asked again after six months). They never reach us.

The list page keeps your key (il-key), which it sends to us only to check it, and your language (il-lang), which never reaches us.

Who else is involved

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security. Sign-ups go to our server on Cloudflare, which handles your IP address to deliver the request. Links to Copart, the Chrome Web Store and Telegram lead to sites with their own policies.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of data about you, object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your data protection authority. We cannot pick an individual out of Google Analytics reports; the quickest way to stop collection is “Cookie settings”.

Changes

When this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
